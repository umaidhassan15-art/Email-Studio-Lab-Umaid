Loose-leaf tea can look a bit fiddly from the outside. Here's the good news: if you can boil a kettle, you can make it. There's no wrong way to start.

Your first box from The Steep Club comes with everything you need, so here's the whole method.

**1. Check the card.**
Every first box has a steeping card. It tells you the water temperature and how long to steep each tea. ("Steep" just means leaving the leaves in hot water.)

**2. Fill the strainer.**
Put a spoonful of tea into the small reusable strainer that comes in your box, and pop it in your mug.

**3. Pour and wait.**
Pour on the water, wait the time on the card, then lift the strainer out. That's it. Your calm afternoon cup is ready.

If you'd like it stronger or lighter next time, steep it a little longer or shorter. You'll find your favourite quickly.
