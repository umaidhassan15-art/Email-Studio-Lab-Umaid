**Rules**

- Stop sending launch emails to anyone as soon as they start their box or buy a gift.
- Gift buyers get Email 1 only from this set. Emails 2 to 4 talk about the 31 December free-shipping date, which doesn't apply to gifts.
- Don't send to anyone who has unsubscribed.
- Nobody comes back into the sequence. After 3 January the launch is over.

## What to watch

Healthy levels come from part 8 of `plan.md`:

- **Main number:** members who start their box from each email. Use a different tracking tag on each email's link.
- **Click rate:** 5% or more.
- **Open rate:** 40% or more (a rough guide only).
- **Unsubscribes:** under 1% per email.
- **Check every Monday.** If week 1 is under 100 members, make Email 2 shorter and more direct.

## Before you send

1. Run each email through `/marketing:brand-review`.
2. Send yourself a test copy. Check the link, the mobile view and the sign-off.
3. Make sure the landing page says the same things: $24 a month, $4.50 shipping, free first-box shipping by 31 December, first box ships 5 January, renewal on the 1st.
