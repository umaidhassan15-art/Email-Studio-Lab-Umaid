**Rules**

- Stop sending launch emails to anyone as soon as they start their box or buy a gift.
- Gift buyers never get emails 02, 03 or 04. Those talk about the 31 December free-shipping date, which doesn't apply to gifts.
- Beginners and daily drinkers never get the gift emails (06, 08, 09, 10).
- Don't send to anyone who has unsubscribed.
- Nobody comes back into the sequence. After 3 January the launch is over.

## What to watch

Healthy levels come from part 8 of `plan.md`:

- **Main number:** members who start their box from each email. Use a different tracking tag on each email's link.
- **Gift orders:** count them on their own line (3-month, 6-month, e-gift).
- **Click rate:** 5% or more.
- **Open rate:** 40% or more (a rough guide only).
- **Unsubscribes:** under 1% per email.
- **Check every Monday.** If week 1 is under 100 members, make email 02 shorter and more direct.

## Before you send

1. Run each email through `/marketing:brand-review`.
2. Check the e-gift (email 09) on the shop: is it the same 3- or 6-month gift, with free shipping and no auto-renewal? The email says so, but the plan doesn't say it in so many words.
3. Send yourself a test copy of each. Check the link, the mobile view and the sign-off.
4. Make sure the landing page says the same things as the emails, including the gift prices and the 18 December date.
