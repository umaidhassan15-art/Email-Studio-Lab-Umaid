# The Steep Club Launch: Campaign Plan

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** *Your Afternoon Cup*

**In one sentence:** We open The Steep Club to our 2,000 waitlist members and help each group find a reason to start their first box.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start their monthly box between 7 December and 3 January.

That is 15% of the list. For a warm list that asked to hear from us, this is possible, but it's at the high end. We should be ready for a result between 150 and 300 (see part 9).

**Other goals:**

- Keep unsubscribes low (under 1% per email), so the list is still healthy after launch.
- Learn which group joins most, and which email gets them there. This shapes the next campaign.
- Get a few real member quotes we can use later (only real ones, with permission).

---

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 2,000 people on our waitlist. They already raised their hand, so this is the "decide" stage, not the "who are you?" stage. Our job is to answer their doubts and make starting easy.

All three groups are practical and a little sceptical. They don't trust big health promises. They like friendly, honest brands.

| Group | Who they are | What they want | What holds them back |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** (our main group) | 28 to 40, work full-time, often from home. Two or three coffees a day. | A calmer afternoon. A small treat. Not having to choose from a huge menu. | Loose-leaf tea looks fiddly. Worry about "doing it wrong". Unsure about caffeine. |
| **Gift buyers** | Looking for a thoughtful present. | Something that feels personal and keeps giving. Arrives on time. | Will it arrive before Christmas? Will the person like it? How does gifting work? |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | Already drink tea every day, mostly supermarket bags. | Better taste. Some variety. | Is it worth the price? Is it a hassle to cancel? |

**Group sizes:** [TBC]. We need to check the waitlist data. If the sign-up form didn't ask people which group they're in, see "Fixing missing group data" in part 9.

**Where they are:** In their inbox, during a busy, screen-heavy day. December inboxes are very full. Our emails need to be short, calm and easy to spot.

## 3. What we want to say

**Core message:**
> A small box of good tea each month, and a calm moment in your afternoon.

**Supporting messages**

| # | Message | Mainly for | Proof we can use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **It's easy.** We pick the teas. Each one comes with simple steep times. No kit or know-how needed. | Beginners | The steeping card or guide in the box [TBC: confirm there is one]. |
| 2 | **A calm cup for your afternoon.** A small ritual that swaps one coffee for something gentler. | Beginners, daily drinkers | Honest taste and moment language. Green and white teas are "lower in caffeine than coffee". |
| 3 | **A gift that keeps arriving.** A present that turns up every month, not just once. | Gift buyers | How gifting works and real delivery dates [TBC]. |
| 4 | **You stay in control.** Your box renews monthly. You can skip or cancel from your account anytime before the next one ships. | Everyone, especially daily drinkers | Only say this if it's really one click in the account [TBC: test it]. |

**Call to action (one per email):** "Start your box" or "See what's in your first box". For gift buyers: "Send a box as a gift".

**What we will not say:** No health claims, no "caffeine-free" for tea-leaf teas, no "rare" or "exclusive", no made-up savings, and no "last chance" or countdowns. Full list in `brand/voice.md`, parts 3 and 4.

**Word note:** In this plan we sometimes say "join" or "start". In the emails we always say **The Steep Club**, **your box** or **your monthly box**, never "subscription", and **members** or "you", never "customers".

## 4. Channel plan

We are using email only. That suits this campaign: the list already wants to hear from us, and it costs almost nothing to send.

| Channel | Why it fits | What we make | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Launch emails** (to the waitlist) | The list is warm and asked to be told when we open. | 6 to 7 short emails, split by group. | Medium |
| **Automatic emails** (sent by the shop when someone joins) | Every new member needs a warm welcome. Every gift buyer needs a clear confirmation. | Welcome email, gift order confirmation, skip and cancel confirmations. | Low (write once) |
| **Landing page** (where every email links to) | Not a channel itself, but every email points here. It must be clear and match what the emails say. | Check the copy: price, what's inside, renewal and cancelling, gift option. | Low to medium |

**Not used:** paid ads, social, PR. You can add these later if the results suggest it.

**Sending:** You send every email by hand, outside the studio. This plan only says what to send and when.

## 7. How we measure success

**Main number:** Members who start their box between 7 December and 3 January. **Target: 300.**

**Rough weekly targets (to check we're on track):**

| Week | New members | Running total |
|---|---|---|
| 1 (launch) | 130 | 130 |
| 2 | 80 | 210 |
| 3 (Christmas, quiet) | 30 | 240 |
| 4 (new year) | 60 | 300 |

**Other numbers to watch:**

| Number | What it tells us | How to track | Healthy level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joins per email | Which email works | A different tracking tag on each email's link (UTM tags) | Most from E1 |
| Click rate | Whether the email made people curious | Email tool reports | 5% or more |
| Open rate | Whether subject lines work | Email tool reports (rough guide only, since some apps open emails automatically) | 40% or more on a warm list |
| Joins by group | Which group responds best | Group tag on each link | Compare the three |
| Unsubscribe rate | Whether we're emailing too much | Email tool reports | Under 1% per email |
| Gift orders | How the gift group is doing | Shop orders | Counted separately |

**When to check:**

- Every Monday: count new members and compare with the weekly target above.
- Fri 11 Dec: first real check. If we're well under 130, change the week 2 emails (see part 9).
- Mon 4 January: final count and a short write-up in `notes/`.

## 9. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What could happen | What we'll do |
|---|---|---|
| **The goal is high** | 15% of the list is ambitious. December is busy. | Track every Monday. If week 1 is under 100, make E3 shorter and more direct, and send it to everyone who hasn't joined. Agree now that 200 or more is still a good launch. |
| **Missing group data** | We may not know who's a beginner, a gift buyer or a daily drinker. | Add one line to E1: "Which sounds like you?" with three links. Each link tags the person. Anyone who doesn't click gets the beginner emails, since that's our main group. |
| **Christmas delivery** | Gift buyers order too late, or the box arrives after Christmas. | Get a real order-by date from the courier before launch. State it plainly. Never invent one. |
| **Busy inboxes** | Emails get lost or feel like too much. | Few emails, short ones, a quiet Christmas week, and stop emailing people as soon as they join. |
| **Cancelling isn't one click** | Then "skip or cancel anytime" isn't true, and it's against our rules. | Test it on 2 December. If it's not simple, either fix it or describe the real steps honestly. |
| **Promises slip in** | A draft says "detox", "caffeine-free" or "last chance". | Run every email through `/marketing:brand-review` before you send it. |
