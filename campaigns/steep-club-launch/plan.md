# The Steep Club Launch: Campaign Plan

**Status:** Draft plan, with the box facts confirmed. Nothing has been sent.
**Dates:** Monday 7 December 2026 to Sunday 3 January 2027 (4 weeks).
**Channel:** Email only. No paid ads.
**Written:** 4 November 2026. **Updated:** 4 November 2026 with your answers.

> **Before you read on:** almost every fact is now confirmed (see part 11). One small question is left, marked **[TBC]**. We must not guess it in any email.

---

## 1. Campaign overview

**Campaign name:** *Your Afternoon Cup*

**In one sentence:** We open The Steep Club to our 2,000 waitlist members and help each group find a reason to start their first box.

**Main goal:** 300 waitlist members start their monthly box between 7 December and 3 January.

- **Target:** 300 (15% of the list).
- **Good result:** 200 (10% of the list).

For a warm list that asked to hear from us, 300 is possible, but it's at the high end in a busy December.

**Other goals:**

- Keep unsubscribes low (under 1% per email), so the list is still healthy after launch.
- Learn which group joins most, and which email gets them there. This shapes the next campaign.
- Get a few real member quotes we can use later (only real ones, with permission).

---

## 2. Who we are writing to

About 2,000 people on our waitlist. They already raised their hand, so this is the "decide" stage. Our job is to answer their doubts and make starting easy.

Every person already has a group, because the sign-up form asked "Which sounds like you?".

All three groups are practical and a little sceptical. They don't trust big health promises. They like friendly, honest brands.

| Group | Size | Who they are | What they want | What holds them back |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tea-curious beginners** (our main group) | about 1,000 | 28 to 40, work full-time, often from home. Two or three coffees a day. | A calmer afternoon. A small treat. Not having to choose from a huge menu. | Loose-leaf tea looks fiddly. Worry about "doing it wrong". Unsure about caffeine. |
| **Gift buyers** | about 500 | Looking for a thoughtful present. | Something personal that keeps arriving. Delivered on time. | Will it arrive before Christmas? How does gifting work? |
| **Daily tea drinkers** | about 500 | Already drink tea every day, mostly supermarket bags. | Better taste. Some variety. | Is it worth the price? Is it a hassle to cancel? |

**Where they are:** In their inbox, during a busy, screen-heavy day. December inboxes are very full. Our emails need to be short, calm and easy to spot.

## 3. The facts our emails can use

These are confirmed. Use them exactly as written here.

**Price**

- Your box is **$24 a month**.
- Shipping is **$4.50**, or free on orders over $40.
- **Waitlist perk:** free shipping on your first box if you join by **31 December 2026**. After that, normal shipping applies.

**What's in the first box**

Three loose-leaf teas, 50 g each, plus a steeping card with times and temperatures, and a small reusable tea strainer.

| Tea | Type | Where it's from | What we can say about caffeine | Harvest |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Morning Assam** | Black | Assam, India | Nothing. Don't make a caffeine claim for black tea. | Don't mention |
| **Jasmine Silver Needle** | White | Fujian, China | "Lower in caffeine than coffee" | "Picked from this season's harvest" |
| **Lemon Ginger Calm** | Herbal, no tea leaves | Blended by us, no single origin | "Naturally caffeine-free" | Don't mention |

*Note on "Lemon Ginger Calm":* the name is fine, but the copy should talk about taste and a calm moment, never about the blend calming you down or helping digestion.

**Shipping, renewal and cancelling**

- Boxes ship on the **5th** of each month. Your box renews on the **1st**.
- Members can skip or cancel in one click under **My Account → Steep Club**, any time before the 1st.
- **Anyone who joins during this campaign gets their first box on 5 January.** We say this plainly in every email, so nobody expects a box before Christmas. It also fits the new-year idea well.

**Renewal line to use** (adjusted from the brand guide so it matches the real dates):
> Your box renews on the 1st of each month. You can skip or cancel in one click from your account anytime before then.

**Gifts**

- Gifts are prepaid for **3 or 6 months**, with **no auto-renewal**. The buyer picks the start month.
- **$72 for 3 months, $144 for 6 months.** Both ship free, because they're over $40.
- The waitlist free-shipping perk doesn't apply to gifts, because gifts already ship free. In gift emails we just say "shipping is free".
- **Order by 18 December** for delivery before Christmas. The courier has confirmed this date.
- **19 to 24 December:** e-gift. A printable gift card is emailed straight away, and the first box ships on 5 January.

**Main number:** Members who start their box between 7 December and 3 January.
**Target: 300. Good result: 200.**

|---|---|---|

| 3 (Christmas, quiet) | 30 | 240 |

**Targets by group** (15% of each group):

| Group | Size | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Beginners | about 1,000 | 150 |
| Gift buyers | about 500 | 75 (gift purchases) |
| Daily drinkers | about 500 | 75 |

*How to count gifts:* a gift is a prepaid order, not a monthly box. Count gift orders toward the 300, but report them on their own line so we can see how many monthly members we really have.

**Other numbers to watch:**

| Number | What it tells us | How to track | Healthy level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Joins per email | Which email works | A different tracking tag on each email's link (UTM tags) | Most from E1 |
| Click rate | Whether the email made people curious | Email tool reports | 5% or more |
| Open rate | Whether subject lines work | Email tool reports (rough guide only, since some apps open emails automatically) | 40% or more on a warm list |
| Joins by group | Which group responds best | Group tag on each link | Compare with the targets above |
| Unsubscribe rate | Whether we're emailing too much | Email tool reports | Under 1% per email |
| Gift orders by type | 3-month vs 6-month vs e-gift | Shop orders | Counted separately |

**When to check:**

- Every Monday: count new members and compare with the weekly target.
- Fri 11 Dec: first real check. If we're well under 130, change the week 2 emails (see part 10).
- Mon 4 January: final count and a short write-up in `notes/`.

## 10. Risks and what we'll do

| Risk | What could happen | What we'll do |
|---|---|---|
| **The goal is high** | 15% of the list is ambitious. December is busy. | Track every Monday. If week 1 is under 100, make E3 shorter and more direct. 200 still counts as a good result. |
| **First box ships 5 January** | Some people expect tea straight away and feel let down. | Say "your first box ships on 5 January" in every email, on the landing page and in the welcome email. Turn it into a plus: a calm start to the new year. |
| **Christmas delivery** | Gift buyers miss 18 December. | Say the date clearly in E2b and E3g. Offer the e-gift from 19 to 24 December. |
| **Busy inboxes** | Emails get lost or feel like too much. | Few, short emails, a quiet Christmas week, and stop emailing people as soon as they join. |
| **Promises slip in** | A draft calls Morning Assam low-caffeine, says all the teas are this season's, or hints that Lemon Ginger Calm calms you down. | Run every email through `/marketing:brand-review` before you send it. Check against the table in part 3. |
| **The deadline sounds pushy** | "31 December" turns into "last chance". | State the date once per email, calmly. No countdowns or "hurry". |
