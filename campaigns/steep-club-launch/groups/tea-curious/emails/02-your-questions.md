Hi {{first_name}},

We opened The Steep Club last week, and if loose-leaf tea feels a bit new to you, this one's for you. Here are honest answers to the questions you might have.

**How much is it?**
Your box is $24 a month. It holds three loose-leaf teas, 50 g each.

**What about caffeine?**
Jasmine Silver Needle is a white tea, and it's lower in caffeine than coffee. Lemon Ginger Calm has no tea leaves, so it's naturally caffeine-free.

**When do I pay?**
You pay on the day you join. That first payment covers your box on 5 January. Your next payment is on 1 February, then on the 1st of each month.

**When does my box ship?**
Boxes ship on the 5th of each month. **Your first box ships on 5 January.**

**What does shipping cost?**
Shipping is normally $4.50. Join by 31 December and your first box ships free.

**Do I need any special kit?**
No. Your first box comes with a steeping card that gives you the time and temperature for each tea, plus a small reusable strainer. A kettle and a mug are all you need, even on a busy afternoon.

**How do I skip or cancel?**
Your box renews on the 1st of each month. You can skip or cancel in one click from your account anytime before then. You'll find it under My Account → Steep Club.
