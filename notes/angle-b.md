# Angle B: Discovering new teas

Subject line options for email 01 (The Steep Club is open). Each one is under 50 characters.

1. Three teas to discover this January (35 characters)
2. Find your new favourite tea (27 characters)
3. Malty, floral, bright: try all three (36 characters)

**Notes**

- These invite people to try something new without making them feel they're doing it wrong, in line with "gently curious" in `brand/voice.md`.
- Number 3 uses the plain taste words from the first box: malty (Morning Assam), floral (Jasmine Silver Needle) and bright (Lemon Ginger Calm).
- None of them say "rare", "exclusive" or "this season's harvest". Only the white tea is from this season's harvest, so that line stays out of a subject that covers all three teas.
- There's no urgency or "last chance" wording.
- Number 1 also says the first box arrives in January, so nobody expects tea before Christmas.
- None of them repeat the E2c subject ("Meet the three teas in your first box"), so they can go out in the same week.
