# Angle B: Discovering new teas

Subject line options for email 01 (The Steep Club is open). Each one is under 50 characters.

1. Three new teas to try this January (34 characters)
2. Your new favourite tea could be in here (39 characters)
3. Meet the teas in your first box (31 characters)

**Notes**

- These invite people to try something new without making them feel they're doing it wrong, in line with "gently curious" in `brand/voice.md`.
- None of them say "rare", "exclusive" or "this season's harvest". Only the white tea is from this season's harvest, so that line stays out of a subject that covers all three teas.
- There's no urgency or "last chance" wording.
- Number 1 also says when the first box arrives (5 January), so nobody expects tea before Christmas.
- Number 2 suits beginners. Number 1 or 3 suits daily drinkers, who want a step up from tea bags. For gift buyers, Angle A's number 1 or 2 is probably a better fit than any of these.
- Number 3 is close to the plan's subject line for E2c ("Meet the three teas in your first box"). Don't use both in the same week.
