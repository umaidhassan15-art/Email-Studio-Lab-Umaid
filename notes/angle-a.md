# Angle A: A calm daily ritual

Subject line options for email 01 (The Steep Club is open). Each one is under 50 characters.

1. A calm cup for your afternoon (29 characters)
2. A small ritual, one box a month (31 characters)
3. Swap one coffee for a calm cup (30 characters)

**Notes**

- None of these make health or caffeine claims. They're about the moment, not what the tea does to you.
- There's no urgency or "last chance" wording, in line with `brand/voice.md`.
- Number 3 works best for beginners and daily drinkers. For gift buyers, number 1 or 2 fits better.
