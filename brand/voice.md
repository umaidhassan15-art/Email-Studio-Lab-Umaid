# Kettlebird Tea: Brand Voice Guide

Kettlebird Tea is a small online tea shop. We are launching **The Steep Club**, a monthly tea box, to the people on our waitlist.

**Who we write to.** Our main reader is 28 to 40, works full-time (often from home), and has a busy, screen-heavy day. They drink two or three coffees a day and want a calmer afternoon ritual. They are curious about loose-leaf tea but a bit put off by it. A monthly box appeals because it's a small treat and they don't have to pick from a huge menu. Two smaller groups also read our emails: **gift buyers** looking for a thoughtful present, and **daily tea drinkers** who want something better than supermarket bags. All of them are practical and a little sceptical. They don't trust big health promises. They like brands that feel friendly and honest, not fancy or salesy.

---

## 1. Our voice in three words

**Warm, plain-spoken, gently curious.**

- **Warm.** We write like a friend who's glad you're here. We say "you" and "we". Sentences are short and kind. No pressure, no hard selling.
- **Plain-spoken.** We use everyday words, not tea jargon. If we mention something like "oolong" or "steep time", we explain it in a few words. We make honest, simple claims about taste and ritual, never big promises.
- **Gently curious.** We invite people to try something new without making them feel they're doing it wrong. A light touch of fun is fine. No piles of exclamation marks and no forced jokes.

---

## 2. Words we use

| Use | Notes |
|---|---|
| **The Steep Club** | The name of the product. |
| **your box**, **your monthly box** | Use these instead of "subscription". |
| **members** | For people in the club. Talk to them as "you". |
| **steep** | As in "steep for 3 minutes". Use "brew" only casually. |
| **try**, **discover**, **your new favourite** | For inviting people to explore. |
| **a calm moment**, **a small ritual** | For what tea adds to the day. |
| **bright**, **smooth**, **malty**, **floral**, **toasty** | Simple taste words. |
| **skip or cancel anytime** | Say it plainly, and only when it's true (see part 4). |

---

## 3. Words we avoid

- **Health promises:** detox, cleanse, boosts immunity, burns fat, cures, heals.
- **Pressure and fake urgency:** last chance, limited time only, hurry, don't miss out, act now.
- **Fancy or empty words:** artisanal, curated luxury, elevate, game-changer, best ever, world's finest.
- **People words we don't use:** customers, users. (Say "members" or "you".)
- **"Subscription"** as the main name for the box. (Say "your box" or "The Steep Club".)
- **Tea snobbery:** "proper tea drinkers know…" or anything that makes beginners feel wrong.
- **Shouting:** ALL CAPS, or more than one exclamation mark in a row.

---

## 4. Claims we must never make

These are hard rules, stronger than the word list. Each one comes with a safe thing to say instead.

| Topic | Never say | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| **Health** | That tea detoxes, cleanses, cures, treats or prevents illness, boosts immunity or helps weight loss. | Talk about taste and moments: "a calm cup for your afternoon". |
| **Caffeine** | "Caffeine-free" for any tea made with tea leaves. "Won't keep you up." "Energy without the crash." | "Lower in caffeine than coffee" for green and white teas. "Naturally caffeine-free" only for herbal blends with no tea leaves. |
| **Origin** | "Single-estate", "organic", "direct from the farmer" or "fair trade", unless we can prove it with certification. | Name the region only when we know it: "a black tea from Assam". |
| **Price and savings** | Made-up savings ("save 50%", "worth $80"). "The cheapest." "Best value anywhere." Hidden shipping costs. | State the real price and what's in the box. |
| **Freshness and rarity** | "Rare", "exclusive", "only 100 boxes left", "freshest tea in the world", unless it's true and we can show it. | "Picked from this season's harvest", only when it is. |
| **Cancelling** | "Cancel anytime" if cancelling isn't really one click. Hiding the renewal. | "Your box renews on the 1st of each month. You can skip or cancel in one click from your account anytime before then." In launch emails, say when people pay first: "You pay for your 5 January box on the day you join, and your next payment is on 1 February." |
| **Reviews and numbers** | Made-up reviews, star ratings or member counts. | Only real quotes and real figures. |

---

## 5. How we sign off

Umaid, our founder, signs every email, with "and the Kettlebird team" underneath. Members hear from one real-feeling person and know a small team is behind the box.

**Default (all launch emails):**

> Happy steeping,
> Umaid and the Kettlebird team

**Variations** (the name line stays the same):

| Email | Closing line |
|---|---|
| Welcome email | Welcome to the club, and happy steeping, |
| Gift orders | Happy gifting, |
| Cancellation or skip confirmation | Warmly, |

**Rules**

- The sign-off always comes right before the footer.
- No P.S. sales pitches.
- No promises we might not keep, such as "I read every reply".
- When someone cancels or skips, we don't guilt-trip them, and we never ask "are you sure?" more than once. We simply confirm it and say they're welcome back anytime.
